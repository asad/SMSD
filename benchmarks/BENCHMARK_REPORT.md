# SMSD benchmark evidence

Use [RESULTS_7.2.0.md](RESULTS_7.2.0.md) for the current local release comparison,
including source versions, chemistry contracts, budgets, validation and coverage.
[README.md](README.md) describes runnable entry points and the
[data README](data/README.md) documents corpus provenance and known defects.

Earlier `results_*` files remain historical raw outputs. Their default chemistry,
objective, setup costs and witness validation differ. They do not substantiate a
universal speedup, a larger chemically equivalent MCS, zero timeouts, or complete
reproduction of the cited papers' experiments. Current performance statements
must cite the release report and its explicitly eligible comparison cohort.
